Visa Research, Foster City (Aug 2023 - Present)
Staff Research Scientist

Led research on fault-tolerant distributed systems for payments infrastructure, spanning consensus, storage, primary-backup replication acceleration, and empirical scaling analysis. Invented hint-based replication acceleration: filed US patent on \emph{FastSync} (WO2024026321A1, Belady-optimal cache replacement via clairvoyant hints from already-synchronized nodes) and generalized it as \emph{Ira} (2026 tech report), a primary-backup replay framework delivering \textbf{$5.2\times-23.6\times$} backup speedup at \(\approx 11\%\) primary overhead across 24M Ethereum mainnet transactions (100k blocks). Authored \emph{Adhara} (2026, under submission), a year-scale empirical study of blockchain execution scaling over \textbf{42M} Ethereum transactions / 208K blocks: demonstrated that state sharding caps at \textbf{$1.37\times$} even under ideal offline partitioning due to hub-contract skew, while intra-block \emph{parallel execution via per-block conflict DAGs} achieves a \textbf{$13.68\times$} analytical parallelism bound with zero speculation and zero aborts. Designed and implemented \emph{FPS} (Flexible Payment Systems tech report, 2025): a BFT payment protocol with 15k LOC Rust implementation across 16 crates on a self-designed networking/crypto/storage substrate, with reproducible multi-VM benchmarks.
